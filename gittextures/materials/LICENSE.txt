Files here are cropped-to-square + upscaled (nearest-neighbor, 128x128)
derivatives of the original sprite tiles, prepared for use as seamless
material swatches (grass/dirt/sand/snow/mud/stone/brick/castle wall/magic)
in NovaStudio's GitTextures library.
